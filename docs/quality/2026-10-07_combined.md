# The combined pipeline — 2026-10-07

Every stage that measured as a gain, together, on the full rooftop suite (5 panels × 8
seeds) against the current default:

1. **Compose:** Qwen-Image 2.1 with the characters' reference images
   (`defaults.renderer.engine: qwen_image_21`), prose prompt naming who is in the panel
   up front.
2. **Location:** the place's reference image at a quarter of a character reference's
   pixels (`locations.py`, `defaults.renderer.location_scale: 0.25`).
3. **Faces:** each face repainted with the panel's expression by Animagine at 0.7
   (`defaults.face_pass`).

Against the default, outside the noise: **continuity +0.63 ± 0.07**, scene tags +10 ± 8,
the fence panel +11 ± 9; overall +3 ± 4 (within it). The location fixes bring identity
back from 88% to 90%, level with the default.

Sheet: `2026-10-07_combined.jpg` (top: default; bottom: combined; each panel at seeds
101 and 202). The combined row reads as one scene: the same rooftop at sunset in every
panel, the same Akira, a surprised face in the close-up, the two-shot on the roof, the
pair on the stairs. The default has plain backgrounds and a split two-shot.

**Choosing:** the combined pipeline is the most coherent page; Qwen + faces without
locations scores highest per panel. Locations cost a little per-panel identity (a
lone boy's gender cues) for a large gain in continuity.

**Still weak everywhere:** the drag (25-50%), the sigh (25-50%), standing/sitting in
the two-shot (0%), and characters looking at each other (0%).

**Cost:** about 70-120 s a panel (Qwen ~45 s, plus ~20 s a face) against ~12 s for
the default. Qwen-Image 2.1 is non-commercial; the rest is OpenRAIL++ (Animagine),
MIT (detectors) or as before.
