# Model comparison — 2026-10-06

Can a newer model fix what the SDXL pipeline works around (attribute binding,
interaction, settings)? Five models through the rooftop suite (5 panels × 8 seeds,
`manganation eval`), each with its own prompting: the SDXL models through the full
tuned pipeline (staged tags, regional references, masked prompts), the others with a
prose prompt (`render/panel.py::prose_prompt`). Sheet: `2026-10-06_models.jpg` (one
row per model, each panel at seeds 101 and 202).

By eye (the sheet):

- **Qwen-Image 2.1** keeps the characters the most consistent of all, panel to panel,
  down to the snowflake pin and Akira's blue streaks; draws real settings (a sunset
  rooftop with the city behind); and puts two-shots in one scene with contact. But it
  **copies the reference image**: its pose, its neutral face and its framing
  (`2026-10-06_qwen_copies_reference.jpg`: two "close-up"/"sigh" panels and Akira's
  reference). Framing instructions in the prompt and "take only the face, hair and
  outfit" did not change that (close-up and expressions 0% either way, 8 seeds).
- **Z-Anime** composes unified scenes with good light from text alone; without
  references, the characters drift (Yuki's hair changes length and colour).
- **Animagine** has the most expressive faces and the strongest framing (dramatic
  close-ups, sunset light), but loses the characters (Yuki turns blonde or pink-haired).
- **Illustrious** draws better settings than NoobAI; otherwise no gain, and a
  two-shot grew a third person.
- **NoobAI** stays consistent and fast, with plain backgrounds and split two-shots.

## Two stages: Qwen composes, NoobAI restyles

`render_all(init_dir=…)`: NoobAI's render starts from Qwen's image (`graphs.with_init_image`)
with the whole SDXL pipeline active.

| | Score | Identity | Figures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen alone | 86% | 91% | 98% |
| Qwen → NoobAI @ 0.35 | 86% | 92% | 98% |
| Qwen → NoobAI @ 0.5 | 86% | 92% | 98% |

No accuracy cost, and little visible change: Qwen's style is already close to NoobAI's.
The second stage is the hook for a stage that *does* change something (a face pass).

## Setup

All are trials in `config/models.yaml -> trials` (`manganation setup --trial <name>`);
`defaults.renderer.engine` (`sdxl` | `qwen_image_21` | `z_anime`) and
`defaults.renderer.checkpoint` (an SDXL trial) switch the renderer. The default is
unchanged (NoobAI, SDXL pipeline).
