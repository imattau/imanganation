- **Reordering alone (E1) did nothing measurable.** It's kept because the tag runs
  were measured with it.
- **Tags instead of prose (E2) is where the gain is.** Sitting at the fence went from
  1/3 seeds to 3/3, surprise from 1/3 to 3/3.
- **`male focus` fixes the girl drift (E3):** a lone Akira reads as a boy in 8 of 9
  renders, up from 1 of 9.
- **The LLM's tags (E4) get most of the way to hand-written ones** (74% vs 76%). No check
  got worse than the baseline. qwen3.5 takes 30–60 s per panel because it shares the GPU
  with ComfyUI, then the panel's tags are cached by its text.

## Two-shots: tried, not fixed

The wide two-shot ("Yuki stands over him") still renders as two side-by-side pictures:
`standing` 0/3, `sitting` 1/3. The tall stairwell panel does put the two together.

Tried: starting the masked regional references later (`start_at` 0.3 and 0.5), so the
prompt sets the composition first. Worse: the head count fell from 100% to 17–33%
(two boys, two girls). From step 0 the band masks are also what keeps the two
identities apart, so they can't simply be relaxed. Not adopted.

### Weight types and region shapes (same day)

These were tested on the two two-character panels only (6 renders per variant). The
suite gained a two-shot check that the characters engage (`looking at another|eye
contact|facing another`). **No variant passed it once**, the current one included. Sheet:
`2026-10-05_two_shot_regions.jpg` (two-shot, one row per variant).

| Variant | Two-shot | Stairwell | Count | What the images show |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| current (equal bands, `linear`) | 67% | 79% | 100% | two pictures side by side, sometimes a dividing line |
| weight type `weak input` / `ease in` / `style transfer` | 67 / 67 / 62% | 75 / 79 / 71% | 83 / 100 / 83% | still split |
| pose-shaped (seated = lower region) | 67% | 79% | 100% | still split; the region doesn't make him sit |
| overlap 0.3 (bands reach 30% into each other) | 59% | 83% | 83% | **one scene**, but costumes bleed (Akira in Yuki's ribbons and pigtails, Yuki with his blue streaks) |
| overlap 0.3 + pose-shaped | 62% | 83% | 100% | one scene; Yuki in Akira's blazer and tie |
| overlap 0.15 (± pose-shaped) | 64 / 62% | 79% | 100 / 83% | mixed; one seed split *and* a second Yuki |

None was adopted. The weight type doesn't matter. The band geometry trades the split for
costume bleed: every character's costume tags are in one global prompt, and the bands
are the only thing keeping them apart. Three seeds swing more than the variants do.

**The eval can't see this well:** the tagger has no reliable tag for a split frame
(`split screen` stays under 0.1 on obvious splits), and it counted a three-figure render
as `1boy, 1girl`.

What could work next:

- **Per-character prompts bound to the same soft masks as the references** (masked
  conditioning, or Attention Couple). Each costume would then live only in its owner's
  region, so the regions could overlap without bleed. This is the real fix and
  substantial work.
- **The artist's placement layers** ("placement: Yuki", already supported) for panels
  that need a specific arrangement now.
- **A region-aware eval check:** crop each detected figure and tag it alone, to
  measure bleed and clones directly.
