# Prompt staging and male focus — 2026-10-05

Follow-up to [the baseline](2026-10-05_eval_baseline.md). Same suite (rooftop, 5 panels,
seeds 101/202/303), scored with `manganation eval`. Sheet for the result:
`2026-10-05_staging.jpg`.

## Runs

| Run | Change | Score | Count | Tags | Forbid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | | 64% | 47% | 59% | 100% |
| E1 | shot, action and setting before the costume tags; shots as framing tags | 63% | 40% | 62% | 83% |
| E2 | E1 + **hand-written** action/setting tags (the ceiling for an LLM conversion) | 71% | 47% | 70% | 100% |
| E3 | E2 + `male focus` for a lone boy | 76% | 93% | 69% | 100% |
| **E4 (adopted)** | E1 + **LLM-written** tags (`render/staging.py`) + `male focus` | **74%** | **80%** | **68%** | **100%** |

- **Reordering alone (E1) did nothing measurable.** It's kept because the tag runs
  were measured with it.
- **Tags instead of prose (E2) is where the gain is.** Sitting at the fence went from
  1/3 seeds to 3/3, surprise from 1/3 to 3/3.
- **`male focus` fixes the girl drift (E3):** a lone Akira reads as a boy in 8 of 9
  renders, up from 1 of 9.
- **The LLM's tags (E4) get most of the way to hand-written ones** (74% vs 76%). No check
  got worse than the baseline. qwen3.5 takes 30–60 s per panel because it shares the GPU
  with ComfyUI, then the panel's tags are cached by its text.

## Two-shots: tried, not fixed

The wide two-shot ("Yuki stands over him") still renders as two side-by-side pictures:
`standing` 0/3, `sitting` 1/3. The tall stairwell panel does put the two together.

Tried: starting the masked regional references later (`start_at` 0.3 and 0.5), so the
prompt sets the composition first. Worse: the head count fell from 100% to 17–33%
(two boys, two girls). From step 0 the band masks are also what keeps the two
identities apart, so they can't simply be relaxed. Not adopted.

What could work next:

- **The artist's placement layers** ("placement: Yuki", already supported) replace
  the default bands with shapes that can overlap or stack (her standing behind,
  him sitting in front). The eval can't express placements yet.
- **Pose-aware default regions:** use the staging poses to shape the default masks
  (a sitting character's region lower and shorter, a standing one's full height,
  overlapping) instead of equal vertical bands.
