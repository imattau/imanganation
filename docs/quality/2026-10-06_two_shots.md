# Two-shots: per-figure eval and masked character prompts — 2026-10-06

Follow-up to [2026-10-05_staging.md](2026-10-05_staging.md), where wide two-shots
rendered as two side-by-side pictures and loosening the regions swapped costumes.

## 1. The eval now checks every figure

`manganation eval` finds each figure with an anime person detector and tags each crop
on its own ([docs/eval.md](../eval.md)): a `figures` count and per-character `identity`
checks (Akira's own crop must not show a skirt). Re-scoring the earlier experiments
agreed with the images:

| Variant (2 panels × 3 seeds) | Figures | Identity |
|---|---|---|
| current bands | 100% | 93% |
| overlapping bands (0.3) | 67% | 64% (costume bleed) |
| overlap 0.15 | 33% | 62% (a cloned Yuki) |

Overlapping figures (one behind the other) skip their `forbid` checks: their crop shows
both people.

## 2. Masked character prompts

`graphs.with_masked_prompts`: each character's appearance, face and pose is a prompt of
its own, masked with `ConditioningSetMask` to the same soft region as their reference.
The shared prompt keeps only the scene (style, head count, shot, shared action, setting).

Two-character panels, **8 seeds** (`2026-10-06_two_shots.jpg`: top two rows current,
bottom two masked):

| | Score | Figures | Identity | Wide two-shot split into two pictures (by eye) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| current | 83% | 94% | 90% | 7 of 8 |
| masked, every frame | 78% | 88% | 87% | 1–2 of 8 |

Per panel:

- **Wide rooftop two-shot: masked is better.** One scene in 6–7 of 8 seeds (two clearly
  interacting, one with Akira seated), and Akira stays a boy more often (`not 1girl`
  failures 4 → 2, his blue streaks 4 → 1). Lost: `hands on own hips` (0 → 5 failures).
- **Tall stairwell: masked is worse.** It already composes as one scene, and masking
  lost the wrist-drag (4 → 8 failures) and weakened Akira's look.

The split comes from the band shape: in a wide frame each character's band is a
full-height portrait of its own, so the model fills each band as its own picture. Tall
frames have narrow strips that can't hold a picture. Hence the default
`defaults.ipadapter.regional_prompts: wide` (masked prompts when the canvas is wider
than tall; also `off` / `always`).

Full rooftop suite (3 seeds; only the wide two-shot changes): **86%** (85% before),
identity 96%. Sheet: `2026-10-06_rooftop_sheet.jpg`. The score barely moves because no
check sees "one scene": `looking at another|eye contact|facing another` fails on every
render either way. The 8-seed sheet is the evidence.

### Tried, not adopted

| Variant | Result |
|---|---|
| masked + every pose also in the shared prompt | 72% (vs 82%): poses meant for one character land on both |
| masked prompt strength 1.5 | 67%; figures 50% |
| masked + overlapping bands (0.3) | 48%; figures 17%: characters fuse into one figure (half brown, half white hair) |

## Still open

- Interaction tags ("hands on own hips", the wrist-drag) are weaker when they live only
  in a character's masked prompt. A tag that involves both characters (`holding
  another's wrist`) could go in the shared prompt; the staging LLM puts it in one
  character's pose today.
- `refine` reuses the render's shared `prompt` for its polish pass, so for a masked
  render it lacks the characters' own tags (they are in `character_prompts`). At
  denoise 0.25 this hasn't been seen to matter; not measured.
- No eval check sees a split frame. A "one scene" check (e.g. background continuity
  across the band boundary) would let the score show what the sheet shows.
