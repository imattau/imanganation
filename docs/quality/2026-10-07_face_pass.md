# Face pass — 2026-10-07

The model trial (2026-10-06_models.md) found Qwen-Image 2.1 best at composing (identity,
two people in one scene, settings) but copying its reference's neutral face: surprise
and the sigh 0%. Animagine had the best expressions but lost the characters. The face
pass gives each model its job: one composes, the other repaints only the faces.

`render/facepass.py`: an anime face detector (deepghs face_detect_v1.4_s) finds the
faces; each is matched to a character by tagging it with its hair and comparing with
tags of the characters' reference images (best overall assignment); each face is
repainted (`inpaint_image`, crop-and-stitch, soft oval mask) from that character's
expression for the panel (the script's, else the staging LLM's) with their reference
through IP-Adapter. `defaults.face_pass` (off by default): `denoise` 0.7, `checkpoint`
a trials group (Animagine).

## Which model, how strong

Sheets: `2026-10-07_face_pass_models.jpg` (Qwen, NoobAI faces, Animagine faces at
0.55) and `2026-10-07_face_pass_denoise.jpg` (Animagine at 0.55, 0.7, 0.8).

- **NoobAI** left smudged, tinted shading on the repainted faces. Not used.
- **Animagine** at **0.55** stays clean and on-model but barely changes the expression;
  **0.7** shows it (wide eyes and an open mouth, a clean grin, a half-closed eye) with
  the snowflake pin, pink tips and scar intact; **0.8** is stronger but the hair drifts.

## Measured (rooftop suite, 8 seeds, Animagine at 0.7)

| Pipeline | Overall | Surprise | Sigh | Identity | Figures |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NoobAI (current default) | 83% | 25% | 0% | 91% | 92% |
| NoobAI + face pass | 83% | 62% | 12% | 91% | 90% |
| Qwen-Image 2.1 | 86% | 0% | 0% | 91% | 98% |
| **Qwen + face pass** | **88%** | **75%** | **50%** | **93%** | **100%** |

- Qwen + face pass vs NoobAI: **+5 ± 4**, the first change on the full suite outside
  the noise; scene tags +9 ± 8.
- Against Qwen alone the face checks move far outside the noise (surprise +75 ± 39,
  sigh +50 ± 41) and nothing drops beyond it.
- On NoobAI renders it lifts the surprise, but not the overall score: NoobAI's faces
  are smaller and its misses are elsewhere (backgrounds, two-shots).

Cost: about 20 s per face (crop upscaled to ~1 MP), on top of the render.

## Limits

- Needs the eval extra (onnxruntime) and its detectors at render time
  (`manganation setup --eval`), and the Animagine trial (`--trial animagine_4_opt`).
- Matching relies on hair and eye tags: two characters with the same hair would need
  something better (e.g. an image embedding of the reference).
- Qwen-Image 2.1 is non-commercial; the face pass itself uses Animagine
  (OpenRAIL++) and works on any composition.
