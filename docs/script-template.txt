CHARACTERS
MIO: 15, girl, short teal bob with a yellow hairclip, green eyes, freckles,
  oversized grey hoodie over a school skirt, white sneakers. Curious and restless,
  always bouncing on her toes.
KAITO (aka Kai): 16, boy, tall and lanky, spiky brown hair, dark tired eyes,
  school uniform with the collar open, silver earring in his left ear.
  Calm, sarcastic, slouches.
GRANDPA SATO (aka Grandpa): 70s, man, bald with a white beard, round glasses,
  fisherman's cap and a yellow raincoat. Gruff but kind.

PAGE 1
[SCENE: Harbor pier — dawn]

Panel 1: Establishing shot. Fog over the harbor, fishing boats tied up at the pier.
NARRATOR (narration): The summer everything changed started with a ship.

Panel 2: Wide shot. Mio runs along the pier, Kaito trailing far behind her.
MIO: Hurry up! It's already here!
KAITO: It's five in the morning...

Panel 3: Close-up. Kai yawns, eyes half closed.
KAITO (thought): Why did I agree to this.

PAGE 2
[SCENE: Harbor pier — sunrise]

Panel 1: Low angle. A huge black ship looms out of the fog behind Mio and Kaito.
SFX: VRRRMMMM
MIO (shout): LOOK AT IT!

Panel 2: Over-the-shoulder shot from behind Grandpa Sato, who stands at the end of
the pier with a fishing rod.
GRANDPA SATO: That ship hasn't come in for fifty years.
[[Grandpa is always in his yellow raincoat. Keep the rod in his left hand.]]

Panel 3: Reaction shot. Mio and Kaito stare at Grandpa, stunned.
MIO (whisper): Fifty years...?

CUT TO: Grandpa's house
Panel 4: Medium shot. Grandpa opens an old wooden chest in a cluttered room.
GRANDPA SATO: Your grandmother was on it.

PAGE 3
[FLASHBACK START]
[SCENE: Harbor pier — fifty years ago]

Panel 1: Wide shot, faded colours. A young woman waves from the deck of the same ship.
NARRATOR (narration): She said she would come back.

[FLASHBACK END]
[SCENE: Grandpa's house — morning]

Panel 2: Close-up. Mio holds an old photograph, eyes wide.
MIO: Then she's... on that ship?
